## Диалоги и задания по уровням

### Знакомство и опыт жизни за границей · B1

- Teacher: Where are you from originally?
- Learner: I’m originally from Russia, but I live in China now.
- Teacher: How long have you lived there?
- Learner: I’ve lived there for about eight years.
- Teacher: What other languages do you speak?
- Learner: I also speak Chinese, and I’m working on my English.

How long has the learner lived in China in the dialogue? Write your own answer using ‘for’.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

In the dialogue, he’s lived there for about eight years. Example: ‘I’ve been learning English for two years.’

</details>

### Знакомство и опыт жизни за границей · B2

- Teacher: How long have you been living in China?
- Learner: For about eight years. I’ve also travelled to a lot of countries, so I get to use different languages in everyday situations.
- Teacher: Which language has been harder for you to learn?
- Learner: Chinese has challenged me in different ways. With English, my priority is speaking more fluently in live conversations.

What is the learner’s priority in English? Write two lines about your own language experience and what you want to improve.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

the learner wants to speak more fluently in live conversations. ‘I’ve been using English at work for several years. I’d like to answer questions more fluently in meetings.’

</details>

### Знакомство и опыт жизни за границей · C1

- Teacher: How has living abroad shaped the way you learn languages?
- Learner: It’s made me rely more on using a language in real situations. I’ve been living in China for several years, and travel has also shown me how differently people express the same idea.
- Teacher: What would you improve next?
- Learner: I’d like to respond more spontaneously in English while keeping my explanations precise.

Paraphrase how living abroad changed the learner’s approach. Then propose one exercise that would help him answer spontaneously and precisely.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

He has come to depend more on using languages in real situations. I’d practise giving a one-minute answer to an unexpected question, then rewrite it in two precise sentences.

</details>

### Один лишний билет на футбол · B1

- James: I’m taking my nephew to a football match on Saturday. I’ve got a spare ticket. Do you want to come?
- Learner: I’m having lunch with my mum. What time does the match start?
- James: It starts at five.
- Learner: Great. I’ll come, then.

Why does the learner ask what time the match starts? Does he accept the ticket?

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

He’s having lunch with his mum. The match starts at five, so he says he’ll come.

</details>

### Один лишний билет на футбол · B2

- James: I’m taking my nephew to his first match on Saturday and I’ve got a spare ticket. Would you be interested?
- Learner: I’m having lunch with my mum, so it depends on the kick-off time.
- James: It starts at five. I’ve just checked.
- Learner: In that case I’ll take the ticket. I’ll message you when I’m on my way.

Which words show an existing plan, and which show a decision made during the conversation? Write a new reply using both forms.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

‘I’m having lunch with my mum’ is an existing plan. ‘I’ll take the ticket’ is a decision made now. ‘I’m meeting a friend at noon, but I’ll join you after that.’

</details>

### Один лишний билет на футбол · C1

- James: I’m taking my nephew to his first match this Saturday. Someone dropped out, so I’ve got a spare ticket if you fancy it.
- Learner: I’m already having lunch with my mum, but I could make it if the match starts late enough. What time’s kick-off?
- James: Five o’clock. The stadium gates open earlier, so we’re meeting outside at four thirty.
- Learner: That works for me. I’ll take the spare ticket and meet you there.

Write a short message confirming the meeting place, time and your decision. Use ‘kick-off’ or ‘make it’.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

I can make it. I’ll meet you outside the stadium at 4:30, half an hour before kick-off. Thanks for the spare ticket!

</details>

### Четыре формы будущего · B1

- Teacher: You have no plans for Friday. What do you say?
- Learner: Maybe I’ll go to the theatre.
- Teacher: Now you’ve made a plan.
- Learner: I’m going to see a play.
- Teacher: What does the timetable say?
- Learner: The play starts at seven.
- Teacher: And now you’ve booked the tickets with a friend?
- Learner: We’re seeing the play on Friday.

What time does the play start? Copy the sentence that shows an arrangement with a friend.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

The play starts at seven. ‘We’re seeing the play on Friday.’

</details>

### Четыре формы будущего · B2

- Teacher: Why do we say ‘the performance starts at seven’?
- Learner: The time is fixed by the timetable. It’s independent of my own plan.
- Teacher: Give me a spontaneous decision and an earlier intention.
- Learner: ‘I’ll lend you my calculator’ is an offer I’m making now. ‘I’m going to take two days off’ is something I’d already decided.
- Teacher: And a firm arrangement?
- Learner: ‘I’m meeting my friend at the theatre on Friday.’

Sort the learner’s four examples into timetable, offer made now, earlier intention and firm arrangement.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

Timetable: the performance starts at seven. Offer: I’ll lend you my calculator. Intention: I’m going to take two days off. Arrangement: I’m meeting my friend on Friday.

</details>

### Четыре формы будущего · C1

- Teacher: Could you use two different forms for the same Friday plan?
- Learner: Yes. ‘I’m going to see the play’ presents it as an intention. ‘I’m seeing the play with Alex on Friday’ presents it as an arrangement.
- Teacher: Where would the present simple fit?
- Learner: ‘The performance starts at seven’ refers to the published schedule. And if I decide during this conversation, I could say, ‘All right, I’ll come too.’

Write four linked sentences about one event, using one form each for an intention, arrangement, timetable and decision made now.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

I’m going to see the play on Friday. I’m meeting Alex outside the theatre at six. The performance starts at seven. Oh, you need a ticket? I’ll book one for you now.

</details>

- Partner: Do you want some company?
- Learner: Sure. I’ll text you after dinner.

What does the learner plan to do first? What does he offer to do after dinner?

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

He’s going to have dinner first. Then he’ll text his partner.

</details>

### После занятия: план и спонтанное решение · B2

- Teacher: Choose one of the topics and tell your partner your plans.
- Learner: After class I’m going to have dinner and relax. If the weather’s good, I might go for a walk in the park.
- Partner: Shall we meet there?
- Learner: That sounds good. I’ll check the weather and get back to you.

What is planned, what depends on the weather, and what does the learner decide during the exchange?

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

Dinner and relaxing are planned. A walk depends on the weather. During the exchange he decides to check the weather and get back to his partner.

</details>

### После занятия: план и спонтанное решение · C1

- Partner: Have you decided what you’re doing after class?
- Learner: I’m going to have dinner first. I haven’t made up my mind about the rest of the evening, though I might go for a walk if it clears up.
- Partner: I’m meeting a friend near the park around eight.
- Learner: In that case I’ll check how I feel after dinner and let you know whether I can join you.

Write a reply to the invitation that acknowledges the plan, gives a condition and promises an update without committing to meet.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

I’m going to have dinner first. If it clears up, I might head to the park. I’ll let you know by eight whether I can join you.

</details>

### Договорись, кто за кем заедет · B1

- A: I have an extra ticket. Do you want to come?
- B: I’d love to. What time does it start?
- A: At five. I’ll pick you up at four.
- B: Great. See you then.

Who is picking whom up, and at what time? Write a message confirming the plan.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

A is picking B up at four. Thanks! See you at four.

</details>

### Договорись, кто за кем заедет · B2

- A: I just wondered if you fancied coming.
- B: I can make it if you pick me up from my mum’s.
- A: I’m going to drive. We’d better leave at four, just to be on the safe side.
- B: That works. I’ll call you if lunch runs late.

Accept the invitation, confirm the lift and explain what you will do if lunch runs late.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

I can make it if you pick me up at my mum’s. I’ll text you if lunch runs late.

</details>

### Договорись, кто за кем заедет · C1

- A: Have you worked out how you’re getting there?
- B: I was going to take the train, but I can drive if that makes things easier.
- A: In that case, could you pick me up on the way? We’d better allow extra time in case the roads are busy.
- B: Of course. I’ll text you when I’m about to set off.

Write a tactful reply that offers to drive, asks for the pickup address, and builds in time for traffic.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

I can drive if that makes things easier. Could you send me the address? I’d allow an extra half-hour in case the roads are busy.

</details>

### ACE TEFL · телефонный разговор целиком по смыслу

Адаптация диалога из Grammar Lesson Task Sheet, задания 1–2. Сохранены все четыре части и последовательность разговора.

C — James: Hi Richard. I’m taking my nephew Sam to his first football match this Saturday. United are playing City and I’ve got an extra ticket. Would you like to come?
Richard: I’d love to, but I’m having lunch at my mum’s. What time is the match?

A — James: It starts at five. I just checked online.
Richard: Then I’d have to leave about half three. I’m sure Mum won’t mind. I’ll tell her it’s something important.
James: What could be more important than a first football match?

D — Richard: Have you decided how to get there? Are you going to take the train like last time?
James: I’m going to drive. My sister is dropping Sam off at my place around three. I’ll pick you up from your mum’s if you like.
Richard: That’d be great. What time?

B — James: We’d better leave about four, just to be on the safe side.
Richard: It’ll probably be busy. See you at four, then.
James: See you!

### ACE TEFL · 1. Восстанови порядок звонка

Arrange the parts from the first line to the goodbye.
A. James checks the starting time. Richard decides what to tell his mum.
B. They agree to leave around four and say goodbye.
C. James offers a ticket. Richard mentions his lunch plan.
D. They discuss driving, Sam’s drop-off and Richard’s pick-up.
Explain how you recognised the beginning and the end.

<details>
<summary>Ответ для самопроверки</summary>

C → A → D → B. C gives the reason for the call. B fixes the final time and closes the conversation.

</details>

### ACE TEFL · 2. Разбери смысл всех будущих форм

Use the reading material «ACE TEFL · телефонный разговор целиком по смыслу».
1 Has James got tickets? What suggests Sam’s mum knows the plan?
2 Does Richard already have a lunch arrangement? Do we know the exact lunch time?
3 Is five o’clock a personal arrangement or a published time?
4 Did Richard plan what to say to his mum before the call?
5 Has James already chosen how to travel? What does he offer to do during the call?
6 Is Richard certain it will be busy? Which word tells you?

<details>
<summary>Ответ для самопроверки</summary>

1 Yes, he has an extra ticket. The drop-off has already been arranged with his sister, Sam’s mum.
2 Yes. The dialogue does not give an exact lunch time.
3 A published time: it starts at five.
4 He decides during the call: I’ll tell her.
5 Yes: I’m going to drive. He then offers: I’ll pick you up.
6 No. Probably expresses a prediction with uncertainty.

</details>

### ACE TEFL · 3. Will или going to, десять ситуаций

Fill in the gaps with will or be going to. Adapted from the 10 situations on the worksheet.
1. Can I speak to somebody in Accounts? — I ___ (put) you through.
2. My calculator’s broken. — I ___ (lend) you mine.
3. That’s why I ___ (take) two days off next week. I’ve already decided.
4. We need someone to come over. — I ___ (send) someone round immediately.
5. The new kitchen’s a bit dark, so we ___ (paint) it yellow.
6. I’ve decided I ___ (work) harder next year.
7. What about a birthday party? — Maybe I ___ (stay) home.
8. We’ve decided we ___ (not sell) the house after all.
9. Did Accounts email you? — I ___ (check) my inbox.
10. Are you friends again? — No, I ___ (never speak) to him again.

<details>
<summary>Ответ для самопроверки</summary>

1 I’ll put, 2 I’ll lend, 3 I’m going to take, 4 I’ll send, 5 we’re going to paint, 6 I’m going to work, 7 I’ll stay, 8 we aren’t going to sell, 9 I’ll check, 10 I’ll never speak.
In 10, ‘I’m never going to speak to him again’ also works if the speaker is reporting an existing intention. The will version works as an emphatic decision or vow.

</details>

### ACE TEFL · 4. Расписание или договорённость

Choose the form that fits the situation best.
1 I’m meeting / I’ll meet a friend at the pub at eight tonight. It’s arranged.
2 The train is leaving / leaves at 15:15 according to the timetable.
3 Did you forget? Two friends will come over / are coming over tonight.
4 I can’t make it tomorrow morning. I see / am seeing the dentist at nine.
5 The Tuesday lesson is starting / starts at ten according to the schedule.
6 We’ve set a wedding date. We will get married / are getting married on 6 August.

<details>
<summary>Ответ для самопроверки</summary>

1 I’m meeting, 2 leaves, 3 are coming over, 4 am seeing, 5 starts, 6 are getting married. Schedules typically take present simple, arrangements typically take present continuous in these contexts.

</details>
