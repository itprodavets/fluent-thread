## Диалоги и задания по уровням

### Короткие ответы и усиление с do · B1

- Teacher: Have you seen Ben today?
- Learner: Yes, I have.
- Teacher: Did you like the film?
- Learner: No, I didn’t. But I do like the director’s other films.
- Teacher: Why did you use ‘do’?
- Learner: I wanted to make my opinion stronger.

Copy the learner’s short answer to ‘Have you seen Ben today?’ Then answer ‘Did you like the film?’ the way he does.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

‘Yes, I have.’ ‘No, I didn’t. But I do like the director’s other films.’

</details>

### Короткие ответы и усиление с do · B2

- Teacher: If someone asks ‘Have you seen Ben at all?’, how can you avoid repeating the whole question?
- Learner: ‘Yes, I have, actually.’ The auxiliary stands in for the rest of the answer.
- Teacher: How would you emphasise your opinion of the shoes?
- Learner: ‘I do like your shoes. Where did you get them?’ I’d stress ‘do’.
- Teacher: What if the question is ‘Did you like the film?’
- Learner: Then I’d answer ‘Yes, I did’ or ‘No, I didn’t’.

Write a short reply to ‘Have you finished?’ and a sentence that strongly corrects ‘You didn’t enjoy the film.’

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

‘Yes, I have.’ ‘I did enjoy the film. I just thought it was too long.’

</details>

### Короткие ответы и усиление с do · C1

- Teacher: Does ‘I do like your shoes’ mean something different from ‘I like your shoes’?
- Learner: The basic meaning is the same. The auxiliary adds emphasis, which can reinforce a compliment or contradict an assumption.
- Teacher: How is that different from ‘Yes, I have’?
- Learner: In a short answer, the auxiliary lets the speaker omit information already supplied by the question. It doesn’t automatically add the same emphasis.

Explain what the auxiliary does in each of the two examples. Write a short exchange in which it first avoids repetition and then contradicts an assumption.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

In ‘Yes, I have’, it stands in for the rest of the answer. In ‘I do like your shoes’, it adds emphasis. ‘Have you tried the café?’ ‘Yes, I have.’ ‘I guess you didn’t like it.’ ‘I did like it. I just couldn’t stay long.’

</details>

### Помнить задачу или помнить действие · B1

- Partner: Did you remember to lock the back door?
- Learner: Yes, I did. I remember locking it before we left.
- Partner: So you’re sure?
- Learner: Yes. I remember checking it twice.

Did the learner lock the door? Which words show what he remembers doing?

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

Yes. He says ‘I remember locking it’ and ‘I remember checking it twice.’

</details>

### Помнить задачу или помнить действие · B2

- Teacher: What is the difference between ‘remember to lock’ and ‘remember locking’?
- Learner: With ‘remember to lock’, I think of the task first and then do it. With ‘remember locking’, the action happened first, and I recall it afterwards.
- Teacher: Use both in one exchange.
- Learner: ‘Did you remember to lock the door?’ ‘Yes, I remember locking it right before we left.’

Complete both sentences with the right form of ‘send’: ‘Please remember ___ the email tomorrow’ and ‘I remember ___ it on Monday.’ Explain the difference.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

‘Please remember to send the email tomorrow.’ ‘I remember sending it on Monday.’ The first is a reminder; the second recalls a past action.

</details>

### Помнить задачу или помнить действие · C1

- Teacher: Why does the infinitive change the meaning here?
- Learner: ‘Remember to do’ is about remembering an obligation before acting. ‘Remember doing’ refers to recalling an event that has already taken place.
- Teacher: Give an example where that distinction matters.
- Learner: ‘I remembered to lock the door’ says I carried out the task. ‘I remember locking it’ describes the memory I’m relying on now.

Write a two-line exchange about submitting a form. Use ‘remember to’ for carrying out the task and ‘remember -ing’ for the evidence you recall.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

‘Did you remember to submit the form?’ ‘Yes. I remember clicking Submit and seeing the confirmation page.’

</details>

### Фильм, мнение и вежливое несогласие · B2

- Partner: Everyone seemed to like the film. What did you think?
- Learner: I liked the premise, but I didn’t think the ending worked. It felt rushed.
- Partner: I thought it was surprising rather than rushed.
- Learner: I see what you mean. My issue is that the characters didn’t have enough time to react.

Why does the learner think the ending felt rushed? Write a reply that acknowledges his partner’s opinion before explaining his own.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

The characters didn’t have enough time to react. ‘I see what you mean about the surprise. My issue is that we barely see how the characters react.’

</details>

### Фильм, мнение и вежливое несогласие · C1

- Partner: Would you say the film earned its ending?
- Learner: I see why it worked for some viewers. My reservation is that the final twist felt underdeveloped, even though the premise was compelling.
- Partner: I thought leaving things open was part of the point.
- Learner: Fair enough. An open ending can work well. In this case, I would have preferred a little more groundwork for it.

Write a two-sentence review that concedes why the ending may appeal to others and explains your reservation. Use ‘groundwork’ or ‘underdeveloped’.

Напиши ответ по-английски, затем сравни его с примером.

<details>
<summary>Пример ответа</summary>

I can see why the open ending worked for some viewers. My reservation is that the twist felt underdeveloped because the film didn’t lay enough groundwork for it.

</details>
